03 · WHAT I FOUND & WHY IT MATTERS

The results suggest that reported developer salaries differ across the four countries, while mean professional coding experience did not differ significantly after adjusting for multiple comparisons. Germany has the highest reported average developer salary among the four countries. Germany has an average reported salary of approximately $78,100, compared with Italy’s $48,600, representing a difference of approximately $29,500 annually. For military families considering overseas assignments, these findings provide a starting point for exploring developer employment opportunities, although salary alone should not determine where to work or live.

04 · HOW I KNOW

A MANOVA using Pillai’s trace on data from the 2024 Stack Overflow Developer Survey found a statistically significant difference in the combined outcomes of salary and professional coding experience across Germany, Italy, Japan and South Korea (p < 0.05). After Bonferroni adjustment (\(\alpha = 0.025\)), salary differed significantly across countries, while professional coding experience did not meet the adjusted significance threshold (p = 0.03295). Games–Howell post hoc comparisons also found significant mean salary differences between Germany and each of the other three countries. These findings do not account for differences in cost of living or establish that moving to another country causes higher salaries.
